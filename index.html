🌪️ About Cyclones

Cyclones are powerful storm systems characterized by low-pressure centers and spiraling winds. They draw energy from warm ocean waters and can cause extensive damage.

🔍 Feature Explanations

  • Sea Surface Temperature (SST): Cyclones often form when SST is above 26.5°C.
  • Atmospheric Pressure: Cyclone-prone conditions occur when pressure is below 1005 hPa.
  • Humidity: High humidity (above 70%) favors cyclone formation.
  • Wind Shear: Below 15 knots is favorable; high shear weakens cyclone structure.
  • Vorticity: Values above 0.00001 (1e-5 s⁻¹) often indicate rotational strength supportive of cyclones.
